Dear Friends,

We have continued to work very hard to continue to make Recreation Unlimited a dream come true for youth and adults with disabilities and health concerns! Our team is diligently at work to make sure that our year round camp programs will be another great year of fun and excitement.




We are very grateful for the generosity of our donors in order to make sure that our campus programs and team are first class in all ways and that we are in a great position to continue our mission of sports, recreation, and education for individuals with disabilities and health concerns as a community respite provider.







If you would like to make a donation to Recreation Unlimited camps and camper scholarship fund, you can call (740) 548-7006, Ext. 120 where a Recreation Unlimited Development Associate will help you donate or send a check via mail to Recreation Unlimited, 7700 Piper Road, Ashley, Ohio 43003, or you may also click the Donate Now! button below, which will take you to the Recreation Unlimited Giving Page. Your support is greatly appreciated! Thank you!







We welcome you to visit the Recreation Unlimited campus to see what we are achieving together. Please call us anytime at 740-548-7006, Ext. 120 or email phuttlin@recreationunlimited.org to schedule a campus tour.


Wishing you the best,

Paul L. Huttlin

Executive Director & CEO

Recreation Unlimited Foundation

Recreation Unlimited Farm and Fun, Inc.
