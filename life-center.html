The Life Center

The 20,000 sq. ft. Life Center was opened July 2008. The Life Center is a major outcome of Recreation Unlimited’s strategic plan to offer sports, recreation and education programs for individuals with disabilities on a year-round basis. The facility is designed with an approach that supports the physical, developmental, social, educational, and personal care needs of participants and their support community. It is the newest space available at Recreation Unlimited, and also the most diverse. The Life Center contains a large dividable assembly room, multiple classrooms/breakout rooms, environmental center and a gymnasium.

Assembly Room

  • Seating for up to 100 (combined)
  • Stage at front of room for presentations
  • 70″ TV for presentations (upon request)
  • Fireplace adds beauty and ambiance

Dividable Classroom

  • Seating for up to 32 (combined)
  • Large picturesque window panel

Life Skills Room

  • Kitchenette for cooking demonstrations
  • Seating for up to 21

Environmental Center

  • Seating for up to 36
  • Large counter for work area and sink

Gymnasium

  • 6,800 sq. ft.
  • Basketball, volleyball, badminton
  • Indoor climbing wall
  • Space available for multiple indoor activities
