Your Program, Our Campus

Recreation Unlimited offers first-class facilities for camps, retreats, workshops, team-building programs, classrooms, and social events. We’re proud to partner with mission-driven nonprofits and support their programming needs while fulfilling our own mission.

Rental options are flexible, allowing groups to reserve anything from a few buildings to the entire campus. Pricing varies based on several factors such as meals, length of stay, program areas used, and any staffed programs or supplies needed.

To discuss your event and explore what’s possible, contact our Business Development team at (740) 548-7006, Ext. 112 or info@recreationunlimited.org.
