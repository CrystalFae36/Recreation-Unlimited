Amphitheater

Overlooking Lake Crum, the newly renovated amphitheater is the perfect location for campfires, opening/closing ceremonies, and religious or reflection services.

Features

  • Rustic outdoor environment
  • Circular stone firepit
  • Large performing area
  • Semicircular seating with wheelchair accessibility for over 200
  • Electric outlets available
